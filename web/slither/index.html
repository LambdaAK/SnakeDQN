Slither, learned by playing itself.

Continuous steering, boosting, cutting each other off. Every snake but yours is a neural network trained only against copies of itself.

How it works

  • Simulator. Snakes are chains of points; 30 Hz physics in vectorised NumPy, checked against a reference and this page's JavaScript.
  • Senses. 32 rays' worth of sectors around the head: walls, bodies, heads, food.
  • Training. PPO self-play, rewarded for mass gained and penalised for dying.
